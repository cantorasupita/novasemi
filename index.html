E · NovaEIS™

NovaEIS™ — Practical In-Circuit Battery Impedance

Real-time cell-health visibility without external excitation hardware, switching networks, or a separate EIS front end.

Conventional EIS is fundamentally difficult to use inside a live battery system.

NovaEIS™ is built on the opposite premise: use the battery’s own operating events, synchronized voltage and current measurement, and built-in analysis to extract impedance information in-circuit.

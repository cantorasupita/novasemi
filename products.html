NB1600

7 - 16 Cell Fuel Gauge, Protection & Balancing IC - Great for LFP and Other Packs

Key Features
  • Use it as Analog-Front-End, Full Function BMS or a Hybrid Approach
  • Fully Integrated Protection + Balancing + Fuel Gauging, Minimum External Parts
  • Programmable Autonomous Operation
  • Accurate cell impedance measurement & In-Circuit NovaEIS™
  • Unmatched, 1mV Accuracy for Battery Voltage, Protection & Balancing Over the Operating Range, No Calibration Required
  • PureCoulomb™, Best in Class Fuel Gauging with Repeatability of 0.02%
  • No Need for RC Filtering for Each Cell
  • Simultaneous Sampling of All Cells and current, Eliminate Load current Noise
  • Programmable Battery Impedance Mismatch Compensation
  • 6 Zone + On-Chip Accurate Temperature Monitoring
  • Programmable Pre-Charge output
  • WDT, CRC and many more self-check
  • WDT and CRC for communication
  • 400KHz I2C BUS
  • Able to Safely Drive Large MOSFETs for High Current Applications, Including Many Parallel MOSFETs
  • uA Standby Current
  • Stackable with full synchronization
  • 48pin TQFP
